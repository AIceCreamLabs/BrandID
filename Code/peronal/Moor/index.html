About

Moor Noir is an architecture and design studio focused on creating spaces that balance minimalism with warmth. We believe in the power of light, material, and proportion to transform everyday experiences into something extraordinary.

Founded in 2018, our practice spans residential, commercial, and cultural projects across Europe and beyond.
